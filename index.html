Full notation guide

Roots: A–G or Do–Si, optionally followed by # or b (for example F#, Db, Fa#, or Sib). A slash note sets a bass note, as in C/E or Do/Mi.

Qualities: major is the default and can also be written M, maj, or major; minor is m, min, or -; diminished is dim, o, or °; augmented is aug or +; suspended is sus, sus2, or sus4; power chord is 5.

Extensions: 6, 7, 9, 11, and 13. Prefix an extension with maj or M for a major seventh, as in Bbmaj9 or CM7.

Alter/add/remove tones: use b or # before a degree, add to add a degree, or no to omit one. Examples: G7b9, C#11, Cadd9, C7no5.

Custom pitch sets: write a root and semitone intervals from that root, each from 0–11: C:[0,4,7,10]. Duplicate intervals are ignored; C:[] represents an empty set.
